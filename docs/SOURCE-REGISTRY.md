## Teacher Load / Class Schedule Foundation — DepEd sources actually used (added 2026-08-25)

- **RA 4670 (Magna Carta for Public School Teachers)** + **DepEd Order
  No. 005, s. 2024** ("Implementation Guidelines on Rationalizing
  Teachers' Workload in Public Schools and Compensation for Teaching
  Overload," supplemented by DepEd Memorandum No. 053, s. 2024).
  VERIFIED (triangulated across multiple independent sources, including
  deped.gov.ph's own announcement page returned by search — not a
  downloaded primary-source PDF this session, disclosed as such): an
  8-hour teacher workday splits into at most 6 hours of actual classroom
  teaching plus 2 hours of ancillary tasks; exceeding 6 hours of
  classroom teaching triggers overload-compensation eligibility.
  Confirms teaching load is fundamentally a time metric, not merely a
  class count — directly informed `TeacherLoad.weekly_instructional_minutes`
  in `docs/adr/0039-teacher-load-class-schedule-foundation.md`.

  ("advisory") as an ancillary task, separate from the 6-hour
  classroom-teaching load — directly informed the decision to exclude
  advisory/ancillary tracking from this foundation entirely (ADR-0039).

  authoritative, but automatic overload enforcement (reject/warn/report)

  metric is produced, the policy is not decided, per ADR-0039.
