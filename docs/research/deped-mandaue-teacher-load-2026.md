## National teacher-workload authority for M09

### DepEd Order No. 005, s. 2024

Primary policy: *Rationalization of Teachers' Workload in Public Schools and Payment of Teaching Overload*.

Verified design implications:

- actual classroom teaching is capped at six (6) hours per day under the normal rule;
- service exigencies may require more than six hours, subject to the policy and compensation rules;
- workload must be represented as time, not merely as a count of classes;
- ancillary tasks are distinct from actual classroom teaching;
- the scheduler must never hide a daily overload by showing only a weekly average.

### DepEd Memorandum No. 053, s. 2024

Provides implementation guidance for DO 005, s. 2024. M09 should keep the teacher-load model explainable and auditable rather than enforcing opaque balancing heuristics.

## M09 product constraints derived from the evidence

1. **Teacher load is a daily-time problem first.** Weekly minutes remain useful, but the engine must expose daily teaching minutes and cannot treat a weekly average as compliance proof.
2. **Actual teaching and ancillary/advisory work remain distinct.** Do not merge advisory into instructional teaching load.
3. **Human-confirmed source data.** Eligibility, availability, bell times, room constraints, fixed decisions and curriculum requirements must be explicit inputs.
4. **Three solver outcomes remain mandatory:** valid solution; proven impossible under supplied constraints; search stopped/no solution yet.
5. **Explainability.** Every rejected or conflicting proposal must name the violated constraint(s).
6. **Independent validation.** Publication must rerun a checker independent from the generator path.
7. **Atomic/versioned publication.** Teachers, sections and rooms must resolve from one published plan revision.
8. **No Mandaue-specific invention.** Local deadlines, eSF7 procedures, checking routes and TANAW rules stay configurable/PENDING until exact Division evidence is obtained.
