# Verification Debt

Things that are **correct as far as they have been checked, but not yet
checked by the means that would fully confirm them.** This is not a bug
backlog and not a to-do list — it is an honesty ledger so "could not
verify" never gets silently reported as "verified".

## [Milestone / date]

- **[Claim that is not fully verified]** — [what was checked; what
  means of verification was unavailable; what the missing artifact or
  tool is; whether it blocks progress or not].

> Every entry names: the specific claim, why it could not be fully
> checked, and the exact thing that would close it.
