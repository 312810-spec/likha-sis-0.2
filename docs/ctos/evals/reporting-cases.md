# Reporting cases

## RP-001 — Lifecycle
Working → Draft → Review → Issued → Amendment states are explicit and valid transitions are enforced.

## RP-002 — Readiness
A draft explains missing source data; missing values are not silently converted to zeros/default facts.

## RP-003 — Issued immutability
Later corrections do not mutate issued historical bytes/meaning; amendment creates a traceable new version.

## RP-004 — Official fidelity
A generated form cannot be labeled official without registered template/source evidence for its applicable version.

## RP-005 — LIS-generated-form boundary
Where DepEd requires LIS-generated forms, LIKHA must not represent an independently edited projection as the LIS original.
