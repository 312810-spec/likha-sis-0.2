## The order itself

**"ADOPTION OF THE MODIFIED SCHOOL FORMS (SFs) FOR PUBLIC ELEMENTARY
AND SECONDARY SCHOOLS EFFECTIVE END OF SCHOOL YEAR 2013-2014."** DepEd
Order No. 4, s. 2014, dated **30 JAN 2014**, signed by **Br. Armin A.
Luistro FSC, Secretary**. References: DepEd Order Nos. 33, s. 2012 and
33, s. 2013.

- **Para 1**: adopts the modified school forms (SFs) effective End of
  School Year (EoSY) 2013-2014 in all public elementary and secondary
  schools, following "extensive review, validation and consultation
  with end-users."
- **Para 2 (table — 16 old forms consolidated into 7 modified SFs)**:

  | Old form(s) replaced                                                                                                                          | New SF                                                            |
  | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
  | Form 1 (School Register — Master List of Learners); STS Form 2 (Family Background and Profile)                                                | **SF-1**: School Register                                         |
  | Form 1 (School Register — Daily Attendance); Form 2 (Teacher's Monthly Report of Enrolment and Attendance)                                    | **SF-2**: Daily Attendance Report for Learner                     |
  | Form 1 (School Register — Book Issued and Returned); Inventory of Textbooks                                                                   | **SF-3**: Books Issued and Returned                               |
  | Form 3 (Principal's Report on Enrolment and Attendance); STS Form 4 (Absenteeism and Dropout Profile)                                         | **SF-4**: Monthly Learner's Movement and Attendance               |
  | Forms 18-E1 (Report on Promotions, Grades 1-3); 18-E2 (Grades 4-6); 18-A (Report on Secondary Promotions)                                     | **SF-5**: Report on Promotion and Level of Proficiency            |
  | Form 20 (Summarized Report on Promotions)                                                                                                     | **SF-6**: Summarized Report on Promotion and Level of Proficiency |
  | Form 12 (Monthly Status Report for Teachers); Form 19 (Assignment List); Form 29 (Teacher Program); Form 31 (Summary Information of Teachers) | **SF-7**: School Personnel Assignment List and Basic Profile      |

- **Para 3 (verbatim)**: "Beginning EoSY 2013-2014, all public schools
  must use SF-5 and SF-6 replacing Forms 18 and 20, and the List of
  Graduates." **This resolves a discrepancy this project's SF5 evidence
  record flagged earlier**: the SF5 candidate's own instructions text
  ("This replaces Forms 18-E1, 18-E2, 18A and List of Graduates") is
  not actually in tension with search-summarized "Forms 18 and 20" —
  para 3's own prose is a summary covering SF-5 _and_ SF-6 together
  ("Forms 18 and 20" = Forms 18-E1/18-E2/18-A collectively via SF-5,
  and Form 20 via SF-6), while para 2's table gives the precise
  per-form breakdown the candidate's own text matches exactly. No real
  discrepancy — both texts are accurate at their own level of
  granularity.
- **Para 4**: by SY 2014-2015, all public schools must use all seven
  modified SFs, replacing the 16 previous forms. "Modification or
  adding new data elements without proper clearance is hereby
  discouraged."
- **Para 5**: instructions and per-field data-element descriptions
  provided as Enclosure Nos. 1 and 2 (transcribed below). Data must be
  consistent with EBEIS and LIS. **Cut-off for recording Beginning of
  School Year (BoSY) reports: every Friday of the first week of classes
  of each SY** (this exact cutoff rule is also independently referenced
  inside the SF2 candidate's own printed guidelines — a genuine
  cross-check, not just a shared citation).
- **Para 6**: SFs 1-6 available for download via LIS with pre-loaded
  learner information.
- **Para 7**: as an interim measure until LIS/HRIS are fully
  operational, MS Excel spreadsheet templates were made available at
  `http://www.deped.gov.ph/index.php/resources/school-forms` (this
  exact URL shape/path is no longer live as far as this project's
  research has found — consistent with this being a 2014-era interim
  distribution channel, not necessarily still the current one).
- **Para 8**: Office of Planning Service (OPS) coordinates implementation
  (contact named in the order: Mr. Jonathan F. Diche).
- **Para 9**: immediate dissemination and strict compliance directed.

## Enclosure No. 2 — data element descriptions (per form, condensed)

Full field-by-field descriptions were read for all seven forms. Common
header fields across all seven: School ID (6-digit EBEIS code), School
Year (June-March, 10 months), School Name. Form-specific fields of
particular note (fields this project's own candidates were checked
against — see each form's own evidence file for the actual
comparison):

- **SF1**: LRN (12-digit, cites **DO 22, s. 2012**), Name of Learner,
  Sex, Birth Date, **Place of Birth (Province)**, Age by June (1st
  Friday), Mother Tongue (19 major languages named, citing **DO 16,
  s. 2012** and **DO 28, s. 2013**), IP/ethnic group, Religion (Muslim
  learners recorded as "Islam"), address fields, Name of Father/Mother/
  Guardian, Relationship to Guardian, Contact Number, Remarks. **No
  "Learning Modality" field** — that field does not appear in this
  2014 enclosure at all.
- **SF2**: Month, Learner's Name, daily attendance (M-Th-F), Total Days
  Absent/Tardy per pupil, Remarks, Enrolment as of 1st Friday of June,
  Late Enrolment, Registered Learner as of end of month, Percentage of
  Enrolment (exact formula given), Average Daily Attendance (exact
  formula), Percentage of Attendance (exact formula), Number of
  students with 5 consecutive days of absence, Drop Out/Transferred In/
  Transferred Out (each M/F/Total), Signature of Teacher, Signature of
  School Head.
- **SF3**: Title of Book & subject area, Date Issued/Returned (with a
  documented code system for lost/unreturned books: FM=Force Majeure,
  TDO=Transferred/Dropout, NEG=Negligence; and action-taken codes
  LLTR/TLTR/PTL), Total Male/Female/Learners, Total Copies Issued/
  Returned, Signature of Teacher.
- **SF4**: Name of Adviser, Grade/Year Level, Section, Registered
  Learners (M/F/Total), Attendance (Daily Average + Percentage, exact
  formulas given), Drop Out (Cumulative-Previous-Month / For-the-Month
  / Cumulative-End-of-Month, each M/F/Total), Transferred Out
  (same three-way cumulative split), Transferred In (same), Name/
  Signature of School Head.
- **SF5**: Curriculum ("K to 12 BEC" or "Restructured BEC"), LRN,
  Learner's Name, General Average (3 decimal places for honor students,
  2 for non-honors, descriptive letter value in parentheses), **Action
  Taken: Promoted / Irregular / Retained** (citing **D.O. #73, s. 2012**
  §G p.5 for the Beginning-level summer-class rule, and **D.M. 46,
  s. 2013** for a Grade 1 K-to-12 special instruction), Incomplete
  Subject/s (two columns: completed-as-of-current-SY and
  still-incomplete-as-of-end-of-SY), Summary Table (Promoted/Irregular/
  Retained, M/F/Total), **Level of Proficiency — Beginning (≤74%),
  Developing (75-79%), Approaching Proficiency (80-84%), Proficient
  (85-89%), Advanced (≥90%)**, Name/Signature of Class Adviser and
  School Head.
- **SF6**: same School ID/Year/Name header, Promoted/Irregular/Retained
  summary (M/F/Total), the identical five-band **Beginning/Developing/
  Approaching/Proficient/Advanced** Level-of-Proficiency scale as SF5
  (not a different scale), Name/Signature of School Head, **Name/
  Signature of DPO/EPS** (Division Planning Officer or Education
  Program Supervisor who reviews/validates), Name/Signature of Schools
  Division Superintendent.
- **SF7**: Nationally-Funded Teaching/Non-Teaching Items (Title of
  Plantilla Position, Number of Incumbent), Other Appointments and
  Funding Sources, Name of Personnel, Sex, Fund Source, Position/
  Designation, **Nature of Appointment/Employment Status** (a full
  taxonomy given: Permanent/Temporary/Substitute/Coterminous/
  Contractual/Casual-Emergency/Job Order-Contract of Service/
  Volunteer, each defined), Educational Qualification (Degree/Major/
  Minor), Subject Taught, Daily Program (time duration), Average
  Teaching Minutes per Day, Remarks, Name/Signature of School Head.
