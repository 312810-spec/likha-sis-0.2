## Governing issuances (found, not primary-source-read)

- **DepEd Order No. 4, s. 2014** — founding order (same as SF1/SF2/SF4).
  The candidate's own instructions text says SF5 "replaces Forms 18-E1,
  18-E2, 18A and List of Graduates" — search snippets describe DO 4
  s.2014 as replacing "Forms 18 and 20, and the List of Graduates."
  This discrepancy is unresolved (not confirmable without a primary
  read); the candidate's own more granular citation may simply be more
  precise.
- **DepEd Order No. 11, s. 2018** — "Guidelines on the Preparation and
  Checking of School Forms." A _procedural_ order (governs the School
  Checking Committee/Division Checking Committee validation workflow,
  not SF5's data fields). Confirmed real and still the governing
  checking-process order as of DepEd Memorandum No. 037, s. 2022
  ("Resumption of Implementation of the Policy on Checking of School
  Forms... as prescribed in DO 11, s. 2018") — DO 11 s.2018's
  onsite/DCC checking was suspended during COVID and resumed per DM 037
  s.2022.
  - **"SCC" = School Checking Committee** (not "School Consolidation
    Committee"), chaired by the School Head, with two Vice Chairs (ICT/
    LIS coordinator; curriculum/assessment). Conducts a pre-checking
    pass on all learner records before the scheduled Division Checking
    Committee (DCC) review. SF1, SF4, SF5, SF6, and SF10 are all
    subject to this same SCC/DCC cadence.
- **DepEd Order No. 8, s. 2015** ("Policy Guidelines on Classroom
  Assessment") — defines the proficiency bands (Outstanding 90-100,
  Very Satisfactory 85-89, Satisfactory 80-84, Fairly Satisfactory
  75-79, Did Not Meet Expectations <75) and the promotion/retention
  rule (≤2 failed learning areas → remedial; more → retained) that SF5
  computes and reports. Clarified by DO 29, s. 2015.

## ⚠️ Important finding: DepEd Order No. 015, s. 2026 — now primary-source confirmed

**Not previously on this project's radar, not cited by the candidate
file, and directly relevant to SF5's core computation.** Full text of
this order (and the related DO 009, s. 2026 three-term calendar order)
was read directly this session from owner-supplied primary-source PDFs.
Record: `docs/form-evidence/grading-2026-orders/README.md`
(`ProvenanceState::AuthoritativeSourceConfirmed`). Confirmed: DO 015,
s. 2026 supersedes the assessment/grading rules SF5's proficiency bands
and promotion rule were built on (DO 8, s. 2015 is listed in DO 015's
own references as a prior related issuance), introduces descriptive/
non-numeric grading for Key Stage 1, and a phased transition (SY
2026-2027 adjusted transmutation table, SY 2027-2028 zero-based grading)
for Key Stages 2 onward. **Flagged explicitly for the project owner** —
see `docs/CURRENT-HANDOFF.md`.

## Official downloadability

Search results report a direct SF5 download link at
`support.lis.deped.gov.ph` (not fetch-verified this session). DepEd has
also publicly warned (per search-summarized guidance) that
unauthorized third-party "electronic school forms" are void for
official transactions — **the authoritative SF5 is one generated by
the Learner Information System (LIS) itself for a specific school/
year, not a standalone downloadable template used independently**. This
matters for LIKHA-SIS: even a verified-authentic blank template is
evidence of _content/layout_ provenance only, not proof that a
generated instance would be treated as official without going through
LIS.
